What happens to your report

  1. We check the photo is newPhotos reused from other complaints are rejected.
  2. We look for the problemIf we cannot see it clearly, we ask you to retake the photo.
  3. We merge nearby reportsSame problem within 50 m becomes one complaint with more weight.
  4. It gets a priorityBased on what the photo shows, school nearby, duplicates, age and rain. Officers see the reasons.
